Battery cold plate CFD study

A battery cold plate simulated in OpenFOAM, with 40 designs run automatically and a Gaussian process surrogate that predicts any design in a fraction of a second.

On designs it had never seen, the surrogate predicts pressure drop within 1% and temperature within 0.6 °C, and it found a design cooler than any in the original study, confirmed in CFD.

Validated to within 0.3%. Adaptive sampling and mixing cheap and accurate meshes cut the simulation cost by up to three quarters.

Parametric Rib Study

A mounting hub driven from Python through the Fusion 360 API. The script generates 15 variants, checks each one can actually be built, estimates stiffness with beam theory and maps the trade off between mass and stiffness.

More, thinner ribs give more stiffness per gram. FEA in FreeCAD confirmed the ranking, with the estimate within 2%.

  • Fusion 360 API
  • Python
  • pandas
  • Plotly
  • FreeCAD FEM
  • CalculiX

AI Structures: a Eurocode 3 assistant

An AI assistant for steel design to Eurocode 3, built together with a computational structural engineer. It finds the clauses relevant to a question, follows their cross references through a knowledge graph, and calls calculation tools for design checks, citing every clause it uses.

I built the section properties module, diagnosed why the calculation engine was not routing correctly, and hardened its reliability.
